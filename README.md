# Hobby-Website

Website Title: The modern Odysseys

Purpose: To compile and discuss the modern depictions of Homer’s “the odyssey” in our pop culture. It aims to appreciate the impact of this classic and shed light to works who adapt it

Target Audience: Fellow literature enthusiasts, fans of the story, and internet historians

Main Content:Discussions about some of the modern depictions of homers the odyssey, how they retell the story, what they add, and their impact to our modern culture
